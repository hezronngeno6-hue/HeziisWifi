Money does not route by router MAC. It arrives at your Till because each STK push is raised against this shortcode with your Daraja keys — the router MAC is a separate safety check that the app is selling on the right router.

1 Your Till / Paybill

A Till uses CustomerBuyGoodsOnline; a Paybill uses CustomerPayBillOnline. Pick the wrong one and Safaricom rejects the push.
Digits only. This is the number the money lands on.
Safaricom must reach this from the internet. Run cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:8090 and paste the URL.

2 Your Router

443 = www-ssl (recommended). 80 = www.
The app checks the router it is talking to against this address. Find it in Winbox under Interfaces, or click one below once the connection works.
